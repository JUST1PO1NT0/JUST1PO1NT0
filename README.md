## Hi 👋, I'm Gleb and I stay away from ✨

I'm a frontend web and mobile application developer, I also specialise in UI and UX design. I'm also the founder of B9 Creators, a retired (studying took priority) partnership between developers seeking to provide small businesses with _the platform_ to sell, and design services to clients in the UK, and EU and EFTA countries. We have successfully completed numerous contracts and left customers satisfied during out time.

I like to work on software that provides UX improvements. My latest feat is _typace_, an app which profiles typing behaviour and optimises search queries by removing the 300ms debounce window. I like to keep it clean 
💬 Transit are my thing. People talk often about removing cars, but that might not need to be the case. I often show up at development consultations to make sure that people stay safe on the streets, in cars, on a bus, train or bicycle. Needless to say, safety is my thing.

If you have any questions, feel free to reach out at tylerdelmas0@gmail.com.
